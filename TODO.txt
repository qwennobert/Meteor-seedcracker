[ ] Smooth movement: smooth camera/mouse rotation
    Baritone turns the head abruptly while walking and mining (snaps to the
    target angle). The rotation should look smooth and human instead.

    What Baritone can already do (step 1, little effort):
    - smoothLook + smoothLookTicks: averages the rotation over the last
      N ticks. Only smooths the camera on your screen, not the look
      direction the server sees.
    - randomLooking / randomLooking113: small random jitter per tick.
    -> Add them as Seed Miner settings ("Mining" group or a new "Movement"
       group) and set/restore them via "configure-baritone".

    Real smoothing (step 2, more effort):
    - Mixin into Baritone's rotation logic (LookBehavior): don't apply the
      target angle at once, but turn at most X degrees per tick.
    - Settings: max. turn speed (degrees/tick), acceleration/deceleration
      (easing), some randomness in speed and curve.
    - Only mine once the view is really on the block. Otherwise Baritone
      breaks the block "around the corner".
    - Risk: Baritone internals are not an API and change with updates.
      Slower turning also makes Baritone a bit slower.

    Goes well with the "Low profile" preset (see Presets).
